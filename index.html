01What kind of events do you serve?+

Weddings, welcome parties, birthdays, private celebrations, corporate gatherings, brand events, and multiday destination weekends across our service regions.

02Can I choose beer, wine, liquor, and cocktails?+

Yes. Build a beer-and-wine package, full bar, signature cocktail menu, zero-proof offering, or a custom mix. Your quote will be based on the choices you make in the builder.

03Can you build a fully custom bar?+

Yes. Our in-house designers and contractors can create a one-of-one bar around your venue, floor plan, palette, brand, or original idea. We can also quote refined portable options or service using an existing venue bar.

04Where do you travel?+

Our featured service regions include Charleston and the Lowcountry, Hilton Head and Bluffton, Savannah and Coastal Georgia, Myrtle Beach and the Grand Strand, and Greenville and the Upstate. We confirm availability and travel details in every custom quote.

05What happens after I send an inquiry?+

We review your event details, clarify any open questions, and send a custom service recommendation and quote. There is no cart and no one-size-fits-all package forced on you.
