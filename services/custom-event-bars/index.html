Custom event bars questions

Before we pour.

Clear answers for the first stage of planning.

01Can you create a completely original bar?+

Yes. Our in-house designers and contractors can develop a one-of-one bar around your venue, floor plan, palette, brand, or original visual idea.

02What parts of the bar can be customized?+

The creative conversation can include dimensions, shape, facade, color, finish, shelving, backbars, signage, branding, guest flow, and bartender workflow.

03Do you also offer portable bars?+

Yes. The quote builder currently includes a six-foot teak round bar and a four-foot folding bar, plus an Other option when you want something custom or supplied by the venue.

04Can a custom bar include bartending and drinks?+

Yes. You can request the bar, professional bartenders, event staffing, and the drink directions you want considered in one custom quote.
