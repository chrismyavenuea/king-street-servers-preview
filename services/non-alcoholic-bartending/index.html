Mocktail bar service questions

Before we pour.

Clear answers for the first stage of planning.

01Can we book a completely non-alcoholic bar?+

Yes. Request non-alcoholic drinks as the full bar direction, or combine them with other drink selections. We review the event details and prepare a custom recommendation and quote.

02Can we add mocktails to our wedding or event bar?+

Yes. Select non-alcoholic cocktails with any other drinks you want. In the notes, tell us whether mocktails are the full menu or an additional choice for part of the guest list.

03How much does mocktail bar service cost?+

The quote depends on guest count, service hours, menu, staffing, bar setup, supplies, and event location. Adding mocktails to an already staffed bar and booking a separate non-alcoholic bar involve different scopes, so we price each event from its actual details.

04What is included, and who provides the ingredients?+

We confirm that in the event quote. The final service plan identifies the bartenders, bar setup, ingredients, ice, garnishes, cups or glassware, and setup and breakdown responsibilities supplied by King Street Servers, the client, or the venue.

05Where do you offer non-alcoholic bartending?+

Start with Charleston and the Lowcountry, including Mount Pleasant, Johns Island, James Island, Kiawah Island, Isle of Palms, Sullivan’s Island, Folly Beach, and Summerville. We also serve featured regions across South Carolina and Coastal Georgia, with availability and travel confirmed from the exact event address.

06Can we request specific ingredients or a verified 0.0% menu?+

Yes—share ingredient requirements in your quote request. We review proposed products and recipes before confirming the menu because terms such as mocktail and zero-proof do not by themselves establish the alcohol content or suitability of every ingredient.
