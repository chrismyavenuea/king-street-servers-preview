Event staffing questions

Before we pour.

Clear answers for the first stage of planning.

01What kinds of event staff can I request?+

King Street Servers can quote professional bartenders and event servers, with support for tray-passing, dinner service, bussing, setup, and breakdown based on the needs of the event.

02How do you decide how many staff members an event needs?+

We review the guest count, venue layout, event schedule, service format, and the responsibilities you want the team to handle before preparing a recommendation.

03Can event staffing be combined with bar service?+

Yes. Your request can include bartending, event servers, the drink direction, and a portable or custom-built bar in one tailored quote.

04Where is event staffing available?+

Our featured service regions are Charleston and the Lowcountry, Hilton Head and Bluffton, Savannah and Coastal Georgia, Myrtle Beach and the Grand Strand, and Greenville and the Upstate. Availability and travel are confirmed in your quote.
