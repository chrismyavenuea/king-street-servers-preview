Mobile bartending questions

Before we pour.

Clear answers for the first stage of planning.

01Can I choose more than one type of drink service?+

Yes. You can request beer, wine, liquor, signature cocktails, and zero-proof drinks in any combination. We review the full selection before preparing your recommendation and quote.

02Are professional bartenders included?+

Yes. Our bar options are quoted with professional bartenders. We use the guest count, event timing, service format, and venue details to recommend the right plan.

03Can the bar itself be customized?+

Yes. Choose one of our portable bars or tell us what you want built. Our in-house designers and contractors can tailor the dimensions, facade, finish, shelving, and signage around the event.

04Where is mobile bartending available?+

Our featured service regions are Charleston and the Lowcountry, Hilton Head and Bluffton, Savannah and Coastal Georgia, Myrtle Beach and the Grand Strand, and Greenville and the Upstate. Availability and travel are confirmed in every quote.
