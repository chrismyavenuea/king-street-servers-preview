Privacy

Website privacy.

Updated September 10, 2026

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages people visit, how they found the site, and how they move through the quote process. Analytics may receive information such as the page viewed, referral source, general device and browser details, and an approximate location derived from an internet connection. We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, event addresses, event dates or times, or inquiry notes to Google Analytics.

Quote builder

The quote builder can save a limited draft in your browser so you can move between steps. That draft may include the event date, guest-count range, start and end times, drink selections, and bar choice. Your name, email, phone number, exact event address, and notes are not saved in that browser draft. You can clear the draft by choosing “Start a new request.”

Email inquiries

The current quote process opens a prepared message in your own email app. Information is sent only when you choose to send that email. We use the details you send to review the event, reply to you, and prepare or discuss a service recommendation and quote.

Address suggestions

When Google address suggestions are available, text entered into the event address field may be sent to Google Places to return matching addresses. You may also type the complete address manually.

Questions

For a privacy question about this website or an inquiry you sent, email info@kingstreetservers.com.
